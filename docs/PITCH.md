# Pitch

## One line

> Eclipse turns any English article into a French context exercise. It hides the familiar meaning, asks you to infer the truth from context, and reveals the evidence behind the answer.

## Thirty seconds

Vocabulary apps give you a word and a definition. Reading gives you context but no feedback. Eclipse puts the two together in the place you already are: an article you were going to read anyway.

It covers a handful of English words with their French equivalents. You infer what each one means from the sentence around it. Then a Truth Card shows the correct meaning, quotes the exact phrase in that sentence that settles it, explains why, and — the part that makes it stick — explains why the tempting wrong answer is wrong.

Get one wrong and it comes back on the next page where it legitimately fits.

## Why the eclipse

The metaphor is the interaction, not the branding. The familiar meaning is occluded. A French word takes its place. You predict. The card uncovers the evidence. Your moon phase moves as the concept becomes reliable — and full moon needs three attempts and two correct, so one lucky guess never fills it.

## What makes it different

**The distractor explanation.** Most tools tell you the right answer. Eclipse tells you why the wrong one was attractive. _`appel` usually means "call" — that's the honest first guess. The legal setting is what narrows it: lawyers file appeals, not calls._ That is the sentence a learner remembers.

**Context gates.** A trap only appears when the sentence carries the evidence to solve it. `appel` becomes a trap when a lawyer and a verdict are present, and never when something merely "has wide appeal". No clue in the sentence means no trap — rather than a trap with a hand-waving explanation.

**Transfer you can watch happen.** On a fresh profile, Demo B does not show `attendre`. Answer it wrong on Demo A and it's the first thing on Demo B. Reset the data and check.

## What it asks for

`activeTab`, `scripting`, `storage`. No host permissions. No account. No analytics. Disconnect the network and everything still works — the French catalog ships inside the extension.

That costs one click per page, and we say so rather than implying otherwise. Standing access to every site you visit is a large price for a vocabulary exercise.

## What it hands back

Press End Eclipse and the article is exactly the text it was. Eclipse replaces precise character ranges inside single text nodes and puts them back — it never snapshots your page and rebuilds it. If the page rewrites a branch Eclipse owns, it stops touching it and says so.

## The honest boundaries

Twelve curated concepts, so an article with none of them says so instead of forcing a weak replacement. French surfaces are curated, not conjugated. The moon is a study signal, not a proficiency score, and is never presented as one.
