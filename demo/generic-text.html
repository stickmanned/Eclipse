City planners study how people move through public spaces during the morning rush. They compare walking routes, transit schedules, and street crossings so future changes can make each journey safer and easier to understand.
Researchers observe the same streets in the evening, when restaurants become busy and delivery vehicles compete for limited space. Their notes describe patterns that are easy to miss during a short visit.
Residents then discuss the evidence at an open meeting and identify the improvements they consider most useful. The final report records those priorities before construction begins.
